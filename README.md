
# ClipKart E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

ClipKart E-Commerce Sales Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance across products, categories, cities, and months.

The project uses data analysis and visualization techniques to identify revenue trends, top-performing products, category performance, city-level performance, and opportunities for business improvement.

The final solution includes interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

An e-commerce business generates sales data across multiple products, categories, cities, and time periods. However, raw sales data alone does not provide clear visibility into business performance.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights by answering questions such as:

- What is the overall revenue performance?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories contribute most to sales?
- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- How does average order value vary across cities?
- How does revenue change month over month?
- Which periods show unusually high or low sales performance?
- Where should the business focus its sales and marketing efforts?

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

### Overall Performance

ClipKart generated approximately **6.16M in total revenue** with approximately **1K orders** and **2.47K units sold**.

### Product Performance

**Phone** is the highest-revenue product in the current analysis, generating approximately **0.84M** in revenue.

### Category Performance

Revenue is relatively balanced across Clothing, Electronics, and Groceries, with each category contributing approximately **2.0M–2.1M**.

### City Performance

**Chennai** is the highest-revenue city among the cities displayed, generating approximately **1.4M**.

### Average Order Value

**Mumbai** records the highest average order value among the displayed cities at approximately **6.6K**, while Delhi records the lowest at approximately **5.5K**.

### Monthly Performance

**August** records the highest monthly revenue at approximately **0.64M**, while **June** records the lowest at approximately **0.40M**.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, ClipKart could:

1. Prioritize high-performing products such as Phone through inventory planning and targeted promotions.
2. Continue strengthening sales performance in high-revenue cities such as Chennai.
3. Investigate the factors contributing to the June revenue decline.
4. Explore strategies to increase average order value in lower-AOV cities.
5. Maintain a balanced category portfolio while identifying opportunities for category-specific growth.
6. Use monthly sales trends for inventory, marketing, and promotional planning.
